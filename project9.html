Tracking the Rain

Amid a climate crisis, Margaret Atwood writes about a future Earth devoid of life in "Tracking the Rain." As people aimlessly scroll through their feeds, the poem calls out through radiating typographic layouts on Instagram Stories, asking when we will finally take action for our planet.
