Homunculus: A manual to extract the illustrator inside of you

In a competitive and oversaturated market, young illustrators like me are placed under pressure to distinguish ourselves with an individual art style. So if you can't develop one immediately, "Homunculus" is a satirical manual that can help forcefully extract an art style from you.
