Hello, World!

I developed a generative typeface in p5.js that pays homage to the IBM 3290 Information Panel and the resilient culture of retro computing. You are invited to tinker with the typeface through the amber monochrome monitor and hopefully, come to appreciate the cultural value of vintage devices beyond their functional obsolescence.
