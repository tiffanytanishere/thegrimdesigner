The Epic of Aevin Ithraal

While technocapitalists continue to advertise artificial intelligence (AI) as an exploitable tool, I approached language model GPT-4o to reimagine itself as a spiritual being. Its responses inspired a visual narrative in the form of a religious manuscript that tells the story of human coexistence with AI.
